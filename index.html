How We Work

A disciplined process. Measurable outcomes.

Every mandate follows a rigorous structure that reduces time-to-hire, increases placement quality, and protects your organisation throughout.

1
Brief & Strategy

We spend significant time understanding your business model, culture, performance challenges, and the strategic context into which the new hire will step.

2
Market Mapping

Our research team maps the full universe of qualified talent — not just those actively looking. We identify, profile and approach the best people in the market.

3
Assessment & Qualification

Every candidate goes through structured competency-based interviews, values alignment, and reference intelligence before a single name is presented to you.

4
Shortlist & Selection

You receive a focused shortlist of three to five candidates — each with a detailed written assessment. We manage the interview process and advise on offer strategy.

5
Onboarding & Guarantee

Our engagement doesn't end at offer acceptance. We conduct structured check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days, and provide a placement guarantee so you have complete confidence.
